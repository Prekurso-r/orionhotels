The rooms

Built into the hill, not onto it.

The east wing is cut into the slope, so half of every room is underground and holds its temperature without help. Above ground, everything faces away from the valley road. Rates are per night for two, and include the baths, breakfast, and one hour at the refractor.

In every room

The same nine things, whatever you pay.

01

Amber light

Every fitting dims to 1800 K after sunset. Your eyes stay dark-adapted; step outside and the sky is already there.

02

No screen

There is no television in any room. There is a radio, a record player, and a shelf that housekeeping restocks to taste.

03

Spring water

From the same source as the baths, at the tap, cold. Nothing at Orion arrives in a plastic bottle.

04

A star chart

Printed the morning you arrive for the sky you will actually get that night, with the good things circled.

05

Linen and alpaca

Woven in Antofagasta. The nights here run to four degrees in July, and the beds are made for it.

06

A red torch

On the hook by the door. Take it out with you. White light on the terrace is the one thing that will get you spoken to.

Availability

New moons book eleven months out.

Full moons, rather less so — and the house is arguably better in moonlight.
