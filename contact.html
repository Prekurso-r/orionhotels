Getting here

It is meant to be slightly difficult.

Darkness this good is only found a long way from everything, which is the whole point and also the whole inconvenience. We meet every arrival.

01

By air

Fly to Calama (CJC), two hours from Santiago. We collect you at arrivals — look for the amber sign, not a name board. Ninety minutes to the house.

02

By road

Route 23 to the Camino del Alba turning, then eleven kilometres of graded track. Any car will do it in daylight. Please do not attempt it after dark on your first visit.

03

The altitude

2,410 metres. Most guests feel it for half a day. Arrive hydrated, go slowly on the first evening, and leave the dome for your second night if you can.

04

When to come

April to September for the deep sky and cold, clear nights. December to February is warmer, greener and busier. New moons book eleven months ahead.
